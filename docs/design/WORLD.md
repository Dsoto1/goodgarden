# World

## Homestead
Child's room, kitchen, yard, personal garden, storage/shelves, animals. Practical life, organization, measurement, food preparation, responsibility.

## Village/Valley
Neighbors, workshop, small market, communal spaces, paths, bridge, river. Reading, arithmetic, geometry, relationships, service, local stories.

## Farm & Mill
Crops, animals, grain, weights, cycles, production, food systems, stewardship, multiplication/division contexts.

## Forest/Grove
Plants, animals, habitats, classification, observation, seasons, creation and wonder.

## Chapel/Atrium
Quiet prepared environment for Scripture presentation, manipulatives, contemplation, prayer, sacred calendar, and retelling. See CHAPEL_ATRIUM.md.

## Bellhaven
A larger city unlocked later. Markets, craftsmen, maps/navigation, library, written notices, architecture, music/art, weights/measures, civic/community problems, strangers and broader social responsibility. A larger church may explore history, architecture, music, and communal Christian life without replacing the intimate village Atrium.

## Abbey/Monastery
Potential later region for manuscripts, gardens, music, history, hospitality, contemplation, preservation of knowledge, and advanced reading.

## Lake/Riverlands
Ecology, water cycle, boats, measurement, fishing traditions, mapping, conservation/stewardship.

## Mountains
Geology, weather, navigation, distance, resilience, observation; later-game exploration.

## Coast/Harbor
Trade, geography, maps, navigation, cultures, goods, weather, measurement, letters from afar.

## World Rule
Future regions must introduce genuinely new learning contexts and human experiences, not exist merely as bigger maps.
