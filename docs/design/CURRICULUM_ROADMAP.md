# Curriculum Roadmap

## First Full Season
12 weeks x 5 guided days = 60 primary experiences, with free work and optional real-world invitations.

Each week runs Monday to Sunday: Monday–Friday are guided days (a letter and three jobs: the main work and two small everyday ones), Saturday is a free day for open play and free work, and Sunday is a day of rest with the neighbours gathered at the Chapel. Progress against this plan is tracked in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

### Weeks 1–2 — Creation & Home
Orientation, practical life, observation, counting, simple reading, garden beginnings.

### Weeks 3–4 — Stewardship & Garden
Planting, growth, measurement, patterns, responsibility, animals/nature.

### Weeks 5–6 — Truth & Workshop/Market
Measurement, geometry, money, comparison, reading signs/notes, honest exchange.

### Weeks 7–8 — Compassion & Community
Bellhaven introduction or road preparation, sharing resources, communication, maps, broader community.

### Weeks 9–10 — Forgiveness & Cooperation
Multi-step projects, social problem solving, letters, planning, division/fractions in meaningful contexts.

### Weeks 11–12 — Gratitude & Harvest Celebration
Integrate learned systems: grow/prepare food, construct/repair, write invitations, calculate quantities, resolve community needs, decorate with patterns/geometry, gather for celebration and reflection.

## Content Rule
A day is a chapter in the world's life, not a disguised worksheet. Activities should connect across days: crops planted earlier grow; characters prepare for later events; repaired infrastructure changes routes; letters foreshadow future needs.
