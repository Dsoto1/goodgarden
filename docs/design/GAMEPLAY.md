# Gameplay

## Core Loop
Home/Invitation -> Explore -> Talk/Observe -> Discover Need -> Activity Workspace -> World Change -> Free Work -> Optional Real-World Invitation -> Reflection/End Day.

The pattern may be broken intentionally to avoid formulaic play.

## Exploration Mode
- Direct control of a child avatar in a stylized 3D world.
- Elevated three-quarter third-person camera.
- Controlled camera; avoid requiring constant camera rotation.
- Core verbs: move, interact, inspect, talk, pick/place when contextually appropriate.
- No combat, stamina, jumping challenges, or action-game complexity.

## Activity Mode
Contextual close-up workspace for Montessori-inspired manipulation. Reusable primitives include:
- drag/place
- sort/match/classify
- count/group
- measure/compare
- weigh/balance
- pour/transfer
- rotate/fit
- sequence
- construct
- money/exchange
- word/letter manipulation
- observe/record
- story manipulation/retelling

## Reflection Mode
Used especially in Chapel/Atrium. Slower pacing, reduced UI, no completion score, no timer. Materials can be selected, explored, retold, and put away.

## World Map
An illustrated overhead map becomes fast travel only after locations are physically discovered. Walking remains available.

## Persistent Consequences
Examples: planted crops grow; repaired railings remain repaired; planted trees mature; birdhouses attract birds; restored spaces gain activity; characters remember relevant help.

## Failure Philosophy
Avoid "wrong" screens. Prefer self-correction: pieces do not fit, quantities do not balance, a measurement is visibly short, or a gentle guide offers another look.
