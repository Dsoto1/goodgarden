# Art Direction

## Target Feeling
A warm, handcrafted storybook world the child can walk inside: inviting, pastoral, readable, calm, colorful without overstimulation.

## Rendering Direction
- stylized low-poly 3D geometry
- elevated three-quarter camera
- soft natural lighting and readable shadows
- warm, cohesive palette
- gentle environmental motion: leaves, water, animals, smoke, weather
- controlled material complexity
- strong silhouettes and clear interactables
- restrained particles and effects

## Asset Identity
Generic world geometry can come from compatible CC0 packs. Identity-defining assets should increasingly be custom: protagonist, key NPCs, cottage details, Chapel/Atrium, Scripture manipulatives, Montessori-like materials, Journal/Invitations, Bellhaven landmarks, hero props.

## Avoid
Photorealism, grim medieval styling, excessive bloom, noisy textures, tiny visual clutter, hyper-saturated mobile-game UI, generic fantasy combat cues.
