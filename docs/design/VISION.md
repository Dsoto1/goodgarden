# Vision

## Working Title
**The Good Garden**

## One-line Pitch
A Christian Montessori-inspired 3D learning adventure where children grow in knowledge, independence, stewardship, and service by caring for a living world.

## Audience
Primary target: ages 5–10. The youngest players are only beginning to read, so everything a child needs to understand must also be spoken aloud, and nothing may depend on reading. Support varied reading/math readiness rather than assuming one grade level.

## Player Fantasy
"I have been entrusted with a little world. As I learn how to care for it, it comes alive."

## Experience Pillars
- **Explore:** Walk through a warm storybook world with people, animals, weather, seasons, and mysteries.
- **Do:** Learn through tactile, concrete activities and reusable manipulatives.
- **Serve:** Knowledge solves real needs for characters and community.
- **Grow:** The environment changes persistently as the child's capability grows.
- **Reflect:** Scripture, prayer, story, and contemplation have quiet spaces without scoring.
- **Return to reality:** Some invitations intentionally send the child away from the screen to observe, measure, help, care, or create.

## Long-term Structure
The initial curriculum target is a 12-week season with 5 guided days per week (60 primary experiences), plus free work. The architecture must support hundreds of future days without rewriting foundational systems.

## World Scale
1. Homestead — intimate practical life and early independence.
2. Valley — village, garden, farm, workshop, chapel, forest, river, market.
3. Wider World — Bellhaven city, abbey/monastery, lake, mountains, coast/harbor, neighboring farms and future regions.

Bellhaven should be visible before it is reachable, functioning as a promise of a larger world.

## Product Position
The child's surface experience is a real adventure game. Underneath it is a structured learning system useful to parents, homeschool families, Christian schools, co-ops, and potentially churches.
