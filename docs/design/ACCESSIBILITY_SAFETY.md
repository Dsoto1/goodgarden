# Accessibility & Child Safety

## Accessibility
- scalable readable text
- read-aloud/narration and repeat controls
- subtitles/captions
- remappable or simplified controls where feasible
- generous touch targets
- color must not be the sole carrier of meaning
- adjustable audio categories
- reduced motion option
- pause anywhere outside intentionally protected transitions
- no timed tasks unless pedagogically justified and optional

## Child-Centered Safety
- no ads
- no gambling/loot boxes
- no manipulative streaks or scarcity timers
- no open chat
- no unnecessary external links in child mode
- no punitive loss of progress for absence
- minimal data collection; offline-first profiles
- parental gates for external/account/purchase/settings areas if later introduced

## Content Safety
Age-appropriate treatment of difficult Biblical/historical material. Establish editorial review and content notes for adults where necessary.
