# Asset Strategy

## Policy
**CC0-first, cohesive-style.** Reuse suitable assets rather than spending production time recreating generic barrels, fences, trees, tables, vegetables, tools, etc.

## Primary Sources
- Existing Quaternius library available to the project.
- KayKit as a strong complementary/foundation library.
- Kenney for general assets, UI/audio/props where suitable.
- Poly Pizza or selected itch.io assets only as gap-fillers; verify per-asset licenses.
- Poly Haven / ambientCG-style CC0 environment resources where appropriate.

## Good Garden Visual Standard
Every asset must be normalized for:
- real/game scale
- orientation/pivots
- collision
- naming
- material response
- palette compatibility
- texture density
- silhouette/readability
- performance/LOD expectations

## Custom First
Spend custom-art effort on identity: protagonist, major NPCs, Chapel/Atrium, Scripture sets, learning manipulatives, Journal, Invitations, Bellhaven landmarks and recurring hero objects.

## Manifest
Every third-party asset must be recorded in THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md or a machine-readable manifest with source, pack/model name, license, modifications, and location in project.
