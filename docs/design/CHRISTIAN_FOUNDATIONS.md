# Christian Foundations

## Scope
Initial positioning: broadly Christian and Scripture-centered, avoiding unnecessary denominational claims in the core experience. Content must receive theological/editorial review before release.

## Principles
- Christianity permeates how the world understands stewardship, truth, service, forgiveness, gratitude, creation, wisdom, hospitality, and love of neighbor.
- Do not paste a Bible verse onto every academic activity.
- Do not make Jesus, God, prayer, Scripture, salvation, or virtue into game currency, quest dispensers, collectible power-ups, or scored behaviors.
- Distinguish ordinary gameplay from sacred/reflection experiences through pacing, presentation, and UI.
- Scripture presentations should invite attention, wonder, retelling, and discussion rather than trivia testing.
- Avoid coercive spiritual analytics. Parent tools may report that a material was chosen or revisited, but must not infer spiritual maturity.

## Potential 12-Week Formation Arc
Weeks 1–2: Creation & Wonder
Weeks 3–4: Responsibility & Stewardship
Weeks 5–6: Truth & Wisdom
Weeks 7–8: Compassion & Service
Weeks 9–10: Forgiveness & Community
Weeks 11–12: Gratitude & Celebration

Academic and formation arcs should intersect naturally rather than map one-to-one every day.
