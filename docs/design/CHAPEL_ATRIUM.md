# Chapel / Atrium

## Purpose
A quiet prepared environment within the walkable world. It is not a Bible quiz room. Children approach shelves, choose materials, hear/read a presentation, manipulate figures/objects, retell, reflect, and put materials away.

## Launch Content Target
Target **15–20 interactive Scripture presentations** at launch; never fewer than 12 for the intended full release. Final selection and wording require theological/educational review.

## Interaction Pattern
- choose material from shelf
- carry/place on work surface
- optional narration/read-along
- manipulate figures/objects
- replay/retell freely
- optional wondering prompts rather than scored questions
- return material to shelf

## Sacred Calendar
Future system may gently reflect Advent, Christmas, Lent, Holy Week, Easter, Pentecost and ordinary seasons. Avoid artificial urgency or streaks.
